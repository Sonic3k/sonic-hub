Money

October 2026

Labels work the same everywhere

  • Tap a label to turn it on, tap again to turn it off. Pick as many as you like. Nothing is saved until you press the button at the bottom, and that button says how many transactions will change.
  • A child label counts toward its parent. Pick 🍜 Food and 🍽️ Food & Drink is included automatically. Pick Food & Drink on its own when a meal doesn’t need splitting.
  • Sections are just headings. Category, People, Type and Occasion keep labels easy to find. The old Group section is gone.
  • A transaction leaves To review once it has a category, or once it’s marked as an internal transfer.
  • An internal transfer is the one either-or choice. Moving money between your own accounts isn’t spending or income, so it takes no labels and stays out of reports.
  • Reports add up by label. Total spending counts each transaction once, and each label adds up every transaction that carries it.
