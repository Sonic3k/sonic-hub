The cast

What is given here takes the place of the campaign’s own for everyone who plays, from the next time the game is opened. Her page in the game goes on saying which bot plays her. Everyone in the cast is an adult, so an age is 18 or more. An album is the pictures a player wins from her, one for each game won for points, in the order they stand here; until she has any of her own, numbered cards stand in for them. A picture may be a JPEG, PNG or WebP file. It is kept as it is sent, where the hub keeps its files, and the game asks for it there at the width each place needs.
