# Portrait prompts

One prompt for each officer's portrait, drawn rather than photographed: gouache poster work, to go with the chart and
the views at the stations. Paste a prompt whole and ask for a vertical 3:4 picture. Save each picture under her id
(the name in brackets).

SHOT TYPE, FRAMING, STYLE and AVOID are the same words in every prompt, so that the set comes out as one hand. They
are kept in `art/avatars.js` with what each officer looks like; this file is written by `node tools/avatar-prompts.js`.

## Port Halcyon

### Ensign Pia Lindqvist (`pia`)

```text
SHOT TYPE: Bust portrait (head and shoulders), straight-on at eye level.
FRAMING: Vertical 3:4. One woman only, centred, shoulders square to the viewer, head turned at most slightly. Eyes on the upper-third line, chin at mid-height. Hair and cap wholly inside the frame with a little space above. Both shoulders in frame, running out at the bottom corners.
SUBJECT: Ensign Pia Lindqvist (Signals officer, first posting), an adult woman, age 22. Swedish. Fair, lightly freckled skin, grey-blue eyes, a sandy-blonde chin-length bob with a few strands out of place.
WARDROBE: Dark navy-blue naval uniform jacket with brass-gold lapel facings, cream shirt. No cap. A small signal-flag pin on the lapel.
EXPRESSION: A wide, eager grin with raised eyebrows, as if just caught doing something well-meant.
BACKGROUND: A plain backdrop grading from warm amber (#f6c56a) at the top to coral orange (#e98a5b) at the bottom. Far behind her, faint and simple: a string of signal flags.
STYLE: Hand-painted gouache poster illustration, in the manner of mid-century travel and naval recruitment posters. Flat shapes of opaque colour with crisp cut-paper edges, two or three tones to each form, a few confident dark brush lines for eyes, brows and mouth, light paper grain. Semi-realistic adult proportions and an individual, readable face; elegant, not cartoonish. Soft warm light from the left. Palette close to deep navy, sea teal, cream, brass yellow and signal red, plus her backdrop colours.
AVOID: photograph, photorealism, 3D render, anime, chibi, glossy airbrush, any text, letters, numbers or watermark, picture frame or border, hands, weapons, a second person.
```
