PennedIn

A pen-and-paper duel. Don’t get boxed in.

Opponent
Sheet
You write

Nowhere left to write?
You’re penned in.

Write next to your own last mark: any of the 8 squares around it. Marks are walls, but you can slip through where two only touch at a corner.

How to play

Keyboard: arrow keys, and Q E Z C for the diagonals. U takes a move back.
