# Flat 507 — what the plan rests on

Written during the review of November 2026, when every part of `plan.js` was checked against the pictures in the
archive and then against what the owner said of the result. Numbers are metres in the plan's own measure (x east,
z south, origin at the living room's inner NW corner).

## What the owner said (November 2026)

After the first corrected version: the fish tank, the bathroom door and the kitchen "feel right"; but the kitchen's and
the big room's south walls are **one straight line**; the kitchen balcony does **not** touch the bathroom — it starts at
the kitchen's wall and runs as far as the big room's window; and that window is **cut in two by the study-desk niche of
the row of wardrobes**. The pictures agree on each point once one knows to look (below).

## Part by part

**Living room, 5.6 × 3.5.** Width 3.5 measured twice (video 3:08–3:11, looking through the kitchen opening at the sofa;
the 2012 photograph taken from the balcony door). Length 5.6 from the same photograph: good to ±0.3.

**Fish tank — on the south wall, by the balcony corner** (video 3:23–3:26: facing the balcony, the tank is on the left
and runs away from the camera). A thin wall aquarium in a moulded mahogany frame about 1.1 × 0.9, on a floor-to-ceiling
strip of stacked grey stone; a low orange-brown cabinet under it. Between it and the kitchen opening a short wall with a
small framed picture and the water dispenser (2012 photo, video 4:50–4:53).

**Kitchen opening, 1.85…3.45; the south wall is plain from there to the lobby** (2012 photo).

**The lobby.** At the south-east corner the TV wall stops (z ≈ 2.3) and the room steps east under a lowered ceiling
(about 2.4 m, with a downlight). The parents' door is in the back of that recess; the big room's door is in the south
wall beside it (2012 photographs 1035 and 1036; video 3:34–3:37, 4:47–4:50). Depth of the recess: 0.6–0.9 by two
readings, drawn 0.8 — `EAST` in `plan.js`.

**Kitchen, 2.15 × 4.4.** Its far wall is 7.8–8.0 m from the sofa on the gallery wall (video 3:08). The counter's front
meets the north wall at the opening's east jamb, so the east wall is one counter depth beyond it. East wall, from the
north: fridge (the silver two-door one stands right behind the jamb in the 2012 photo, a mahogany cupboard and a clay jar
over it), sink, hob, a white-tiled pier. In the south wall, between that pier and the south-west corner, the glazed door
to the loggia, its leaf opening inward to the west (video 2:31). West side: the glass-topped table with the food cover.
In June 2013 a new side-by-side fridge stood in the south-west corner; it is not drawn (outside the years).

**Washing alcove and bathroom** (video 2:21–2:29). West of the kitchen's north end, behind the fish-tank wall, an
alcove open to the kitchen: on its west wall the basin under a mirror and a top-loading washing machine. The bathroom
door is in the alcove's **south** wall and opens inward on its east jamb. The toilet stands about 2.2 m beyond the door
against the far wall, a high window beside it (an outside wall: the loggia does not reach this far west), a water heater
above; dark purple tiles to waist height under a wavy border, white above.

**Loggia** (video 2:31–3:07, and the owner). Hung on the south face. Its **west** end is level with the kitchen's west
wall, just beside the door: there stand the steel rack with the basins and the microwave, in front of two glazed faces
(the evening sun comes in here). It runs **east** along the big room's wall — a stainless water tank and the gas bottle,
red hangers on a wall rail, the bent steel clothes rail with pastel hangers, clay jars at the far end — and stops just
short of the big room's window. Bars all round; depth 1.1 is a guess.

**Big room, 7.0 × 4.4** — as deep as the kitchen (the owner: one straight south face; the calendar hangs on a clear
stretch of west wall between his desk and his bed, video 0:13, which a 3.5 m room had no room for).
- His desk in the north-west corner, the door about a metre from that corner (video 0:00–0:11, 1:44). The door is hung on
  its **east** jamb (photo of 25/5/2013 shows it shut: handle on the west side) and opens right round to the bookshelf.
- The **row of wardrobes** is one unit deep (photo of 25/5/2013: its front is about 2.3 m from the west wall) and its
  doors face **east** (reportage of 2008, photos of 2012, video 0:45–0:53); the globe stands on it. At its south end the
  **study desk** stands in a niche of the row, under a hutch, facing his corner; the niche's back, toward the big room,
  carries the rail of belts (photo 1039).
- The **south window** has the row against its middle: from his corner one sees two leaves beside the study desk (video
  0:19–0:29), from the east side two leaves left of the wardrobes (reportage 0:27, 1:21; photo 1039, far left). Drawn as
  two pairs of leaves 0.7 m wide with the row between them; the leaf width (0.33) is read off the frames, the pier
  hidden behind the row is a guess.
- His bed along the west wall, head to the south wall; between it and the window about 0.6 m of wall with the clothes
  rail and the tower fan in front (video 0:17–0:22).
- North wall: bookshelf, clock, and under the hanging shelves the **piano from 2009** (photo of 9/2009) — in April 2008
  a **study desk** stood there instead, and the reportage was filmed at it. The year the piano came is a guess.
- East wall: the wide window with its louvred shutters and gold curtains; plastic drawers in the south-east corner.
- Between the room's west wall (4.4) and the kitchen's east wall (4.1) the plan leaves 0.3 m — a guess.

**Ceiling, 2.75.** Two readings. The photograph of 22/9/2012 shows the parents' door, its knob and the cornice above
the TV wall: the ceiling is about 0.5 m above the top of the door frame, 2.64–2.74. The photograph of 25/5/2013 shows a
person walking toward the far wall of the big room and the line where that wall meets the ceiling: 2.7–2.9 for a
height of 1.50–1.60 m. The first drawing had 2.95, a guess. Heights of doors and windows were lowered to fit.

**Parents' room.** Not re-measured, only moved east with the lobby. The wardrobe and the open shelves stand in the part
of the room that runs on behind the TV wall, their fronts about level with the door wall (video 4:47).
