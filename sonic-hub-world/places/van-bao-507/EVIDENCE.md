# Flat 507 — what the plan rests on

Written during the review of November 2026, when every part of `plan.js` was checked against the pictures in the
archive and then against what the owner said of the result; rewritten in part after the **re-survey of 18 November
2026** (next section). Numbers are **real metres** (x east, z south, origin at the living room's inner NW corner). The
code keeps the first drawing's grid for z and for heights and maps it — `plan.js` says how; `ROOMS` there is the flat
in real metres.

Sizes come from the focal length: distance = real width × f / width in the picture, with f = 0.811 × picture width
at 28 mm and 1.006 × at 35 mm (the video: 1036 px for its 1280). The first drawing took its scale from furniture
guessed at — a 1.9 m sofa, a 0.9 m door, a 0.95 m television — and came out too big; the re-survey uses things whose
size is known.

**Not this flat**, though filed among the family photos: the dark lacquered sofa on a cement floor (2008–2009: the
grandparents' home), the television on a carved cabinet with a tiled floor (Feb 2009: a relative's), the house with the
staircase (2012–2013), the countryside house (Feb 2011). **Not in the archive**: the videos of team 3G4N singing for the
Shane Filan contest (2014), also filmed here — their albums ("[2014.08.23] Van Bao" …) hold only sound files and a few
stills. Before 2009 the reportage is the only picture taken inside the flat.

So only the north strip was too deep (by a quarter) and a little too long; the rooms south of it keep their size and
stand 0.7 m further north. The rooms add up to about 76 m², not 84.

Two corrections to earlier notes made on the way: the lens of the 2012 photograph was first put at 1.34 m by a point on
a skirting board, which made the ceiling 2.48 — the people standing in the lobby would then be 1.51 and 1.43 m tall;
and the long sofa, the first drawing's ruler at 1.9 m, is shorter.

The owner also said how the flat was **before the renovation** (the summer of 2004, between his eighth and ninth
grade, by the school albums): living room and parents' bedroom were one long room divided by a wardrobe and the
television; the front wall stood where the shallow pier at x 1.68–1.94 is now, and the strip west of it — where the
front door and the fish tank are — was the end of the gallery, closed by a green iron gate; a wider balcony room on
the east was taken into the parents' room, leaving the cage. The model shows 2005–2012, after all that.

## What the owner said (November 2026)

After the first corrected version: the fish tank, the bathroom door and the kitchen "feel right"; but the kitchen's and
the big room's south walls are **one straight line**; the kitchen balcony does **not** touch the bathroom — it starts at
the kitchen's wall and runs as far as the big room's window; and that window is **cut in two by the study-desk niche of
the row of wardrobes**. The pictures agree on each point once one knows to look (below).

## Part by part

**Living room, 5.25 × 2.8.** See the table. From the north-west corner along the gallery wall: the **front door**
(0.1–1.3: a narrow leaf that stays shut, the steel rack and a spare bottle standing before it, and the leaf with the
handle; video 4:52–4:56), the end table with the flowers, the shallow pier with the **pendulum clock** high on it
(centre 2.15), the long sofa (1.78–3.38: its east end level with the kitchen opening's east jamb), the rack of snacks;
the tube lamp (2.95–4.17, 2.3 up) and the square ceiling lamp over x 3.6. Before the sofa the tea table; at its west
end the smaller seat, its back to the balcony. All as the video shows it from the kitchen (3:09) and on the way to the
door (4:50–4:54); in the photograph of 22/9/2012 the seats are pulled about for the rehearsal.

**TV wall** (photograph of 22/9/2012, measured on its plane): in the corner the glass cabinet, 0.53 wide and about 1.5
high; then the low cabinet, 1.2 long and 0.7 high, with the television and the **Di Lặc — a small figure, about 0.3 m**
(the first drawing had it a metre tall); over the glass cabinet the **altar shelf**, a board in the corner about
0.75 × 0.45 at 1.75 on two brackets, flowers on it. A standing fan before the cabinet's south end. Downlights in the
corners of the ceiling.

**Fish tank — on the south wall, by the balcony corner** (video 3:23–3:26: facing the balcony, the tank is on the left
and runs away from the camera; 4:53: its cabinet shows behind the dispenser). A thin wall aquarium in a moulded mahogany

it and the kitchen opening a short wall with a small framed picture and the water dispenser (2012 photo, video
4:50–4:53).

**Balcony door.** Four white barred leaves under a row of fanlights, 2.1 wide, from 0.1 off the fish-tank corner; on the
half metre of wall between it and the front door the rail of hooks with a helmet (video 4:51–4:53).

**Kitchen opening, 2.05…3.45; the south wall is plain from there to the lobby**, 1.8 m (2012 photo).

**The lobby.** At the south-east corner the TV wall stops, 1.84 from the corner, and the room steps east under a lowered
ceiling (2.23, with a downlight). The parents' door is in the back of that recess; the big room's door fills its south

**Kitchen, 2.15 × 4.4.** Length by its half-metre floor tiles and the counter (0.6 deep, 0.8 high): 4.4–4.55; its far

In June 2013 a new side-by-side fridge stood in the south-west corner; it is not drawn (outside the years).

**Washing alcove and bathroom** (video 2:21–2:29). West of the kitchen's north end, behind the fish-tank wall, an
